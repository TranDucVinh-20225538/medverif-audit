# Threshold sensitivity on real dose answers (RD, 1451 answers) -- J4-TS

Generated by `scripts/journal4/threshold_sweep_rd.py` at 2026-10-02 12:14 +0700 (repo HEAD before this commit: `ffe0d48`). **Post hoc, exploratory.** No model calls: reads committed score files only.

**Labels.** Negatives = automatically correct answers (n = 1062, 197 drugs; labels automatic, *presumed* correct, not human-checked). Positives = the 12 unit/basis errors confirmed by the **AI** pre-check (`true12`, 11 drugs; AI-labelled, not human), and, for context, all 47 automatic unit-type flags (`flagged47`; 12 of them are parser artefacts on correct answers per the same pre-check, so they are noisy positives) and 93 automatic value errors. With 12 positives one error = 8.3 recall points; every number here is provisional.

**Rule.** FAIL iff score <= t (PASS iff score > t); default t = 0.5 for every score-based checker (reproduces all stored verdicts). AUROC = P(score(correct) > score(error)) + 0.5 P(tie); 0.5 = chance; same orientation as the card AUC (conversion > 1000-fold swap). CIs: 95% percentile, B = 10000, seed 20261001, drug-clustered (primary, as for all RD CIs in the paper; stratified case bootstrap in `table.csv`). Cost CIs re-derive the threshold in each resample (k scaled to the resampled positives); for k = 12/12 the threshold is the *maximum* error score, an extreme order statistic for which bootstrap CIs are unreliable (the point estimate sits near the upper limit because resamples often drop the hardest case) -- read the per-case table instead.

**Did the paper already have this?** No. The paper sweeps thresholds (400 quantiles, best min(QI, US), cross-fitted Youden, inversion floor, AUC conversion-vs-swap) only on the constructed card (Table 2, Supplementary 'Operating points'), and reports AUC on constructed swaps of held-out real dose strings (Table 3B). On real answers (Table 4, Supplementary slips table, Table S7) it reports default-threshold verdicts only. No threshold sweep, ROC or AUROC on real RD errors exists in `main.tex` (v3), the briefs, or any repo report/script (searched `scripts/journal{2,3,4}`, `notes/`, `results/`).

## 6. Reading

- Paper checkers vs true12: AUROC 0.84-0.90 (drug-clustered CIs 0.70-0.96 overall); closed-book correct answers only: 0.69-0.80.
- At t = 0.5 they already FAIL 9-11 of 12 while failing 12.7-28.4% of auto-correct answers. Catching 9/12 costs 11.7-21.3%, 11/12 22.1-41.6%, 12/12 30.4-78.7% of correct answers failed. At a 5% / 10% false-alarm budget they catch 1-6 / 4-8 of 12.
- The expensive errors are the ones that keep the label's number (same-number AUROC 0.73-0.85 vs different-number 0.87-0.96; n = 6 each), i.e. the numeral-bound pattern of the card.
- Constructed contrast: on the card the same checkers rank a correct conversion above a 1000-fold swap with AUC 0.09-0.38 (inverted); on held-out real dose strings constructed swaps reach 0.22-0.44 raw and 0.91-0.97 after UNF v2. Real errors are thus ranked *better* than constructed ones by the raw checkers, mostly because real errors usually also change the number and correct answers mostly copy the label's dose; the ranking does not translate into a usable operating point.
