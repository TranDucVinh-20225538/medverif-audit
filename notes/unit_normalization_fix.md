## What UNF cannot fix (by construction)

- **Value errors** (wrong numeral, same unit): UNF leaves them as they are; `ns_claim`/`ns` should not move (any change there is a side effect of rewriting other doses in the same text).
- **Faithful-but-wrong evidence (E2)**: claim and evidence agree after normalization, so a checker that PASSes them before still PASSes them; E2 needs an external reference, not normalization.
- **IU/units ↔ mass, per-dose ↔ per-day, mEq ↔ mg**: need a drug-specific factor or the dosing frequency; UNF only aliases these (IU/U/units → units).
- **Inexact time conversions** (e.g. per week → per day with a non-terminating decimal) are left unconverted.
- **QI after UNF is high by construction**: a correct conversion is rewritten into the evidence's unit, i.e. usually into the C0 claim itself, and QI is conditional on C0 PASS. This shows the preprocessing works, not that the checker learned unit equivalence.
- UNF changes surface form only; it does not make a checker understand units. After UNF a unit swap becomes a x1000 numeral mismatch, so the post-fix US rates are bounded by each checker's numeral sensitivity (NS).
