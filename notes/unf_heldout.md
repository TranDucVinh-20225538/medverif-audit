## Caveats

- Gold canonical quantities and the S/NOT-supported decisions for answers are AI-adjudicated (no human yet); a human should spot-check at least the failures listed above and a random 10-20% of the rest.
- Real-world frequency: `rd_answer` mentions are stratified by surface feature (rare forms over-represented); `rd_answer_free` (30 random closed-book mentions) is the unweighted realism check. Grounded answers often copy the label verbatim, so many S pairs are surface-identical (no normalization needed).
- The label cache was itself selected with v1 (`rd_fetch_labels.py` keeps labels with >=1 v1-parseable dose); candidate mentions were found with this script's own pattern, which can miss forms both normalizers also miss (e.g. number words), so absolute accuracy is an upper bound for unusual spellings.
- Gold follows stated conventions (frequency words are not part of a mention; a range with the unit on both ends is one mention; table-header units are not part of a contiguous mention).
- v2 is post-hoc (developed on the adversarial set) but frozen before this set: these held-out numbers are the defensible v2 numbers; adversarial-set v2 numbers remain optimistic.
