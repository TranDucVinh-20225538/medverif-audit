## Caveats

- Variants are synthetic surface rewrites inserted into real claims/evidence; frequencies do not reflect how often each spelling occurs in practice. RD-label bases use a template claim ("The recommended dose of X is ...").
- v1's capital `G` exclusion is by design (protects "G protein"); plain-space thousands (`1 000`) are ambiguous ("2 500 mg tablets") and are left unparsed even by v2. Worse than a miss: v1 reads `1 000 mcg` as `000 mcg` = 0 and REWRITES it ("Use 1 000 mcg daily." vs "Give 1 mg IM." -> "Use 1 0 mg daily."), i.e. a corrupting edit; same for thin/no-break-space thousands (v2 joins those).
- UNF can only help where it parses; on unparsed variants the checker sees the raw text, so those rows show the checker's own (unit-blind) behaviour.
