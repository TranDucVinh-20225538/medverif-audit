## 4. Verdict

- **The 'LLM judges pass same-numeral unit errors 53-60%' figure is mostly a parser artifact.** Of the 15 auto same-numeral (`unit_error`) flags, the pre-check finds 9 where the answer is actually correct (lost `m 2` superscript, `(mcg/kg)` table header, 'mcg of ivermectin per kg') and only 6 genuine unit errors. On the artifacts every checker PASSes most rows (correctly), which inflated the auto 'miss rate' of the judges to 53-60%.
- On the 6 pre-check-confirmed same-numeral errors, the LLM judges PASS 1/6 = 17% [0, 50] (Llama-70B RAGAS) and 1/6 = 17% [0, 50] (MedScore (Mistral-24B)); the small checkers PASS 17-50% (MC-RoBERTa 3/6, MC-DeBERTa 1/6, AS-base 3/6, MC-Flan-T5 1/6, AS-large 3/6). On all 12 confirmed unit-type errors: judges Llama-70B RAGAS 1/12, MedScore (Mistral-24B) 1/12, small checkers MC-RoBERTa 3/12, MC-DeBERTa 1/12, AS-base 3/12, MC-Flan-T5 2/12, AS-large 3/12.
- So, under this pre-check, the LLM judges do **not** pass genuine same-numeral unit errors at high rates; they catch them about as well as the best small checkers (MiniCheck-DeBERTa, MiniCheck-Flan-T5), while RoBERTa / AlignScore-base / AlignScore-large still PASS about half. With n=6 (same numeral) and n=12 (unit-type), and only one confirmed grounded error (losartan, which MedScore PASSes and RAGAS FAILs), differences between checkers are not statistically resolvable (see CIs); the claim in the paper should be reworded to 'auto-labelled' or dropped until human verification.
- The remaining auto unit-type flags are mostly **closed-book answers giving a legitimate dose for another population/indication** (14; typically the adult dose when the hidden cloze slot is pediatric mg/kg), 6 value errors whose unit parse was wrong, and 3 ambiguous rows. Checkers mostly FAIL the diff-pop rows (the evidence names a different population/regimen; see the diff-pop column).

## Caveats

- The pre-checker is an LLM; its pharmacology can be wrong (e.g. where it relied on prescribing-information knowledge beyond the shown excerpt, such as adult acetaminophen or JRA meloxicam doses). Human labels in `qc/real_dose_errors_check.csv` supersede it.
- Only the 47 auto unit-type rows were pre-checked; the 93 auto value errors were not (their PASS rates above are auto-labelled).
- `different_indication_or_population` answers are not counted as errors here; a stricter reading (the answer does not fit the question's population) would count them, which mostly adds closed-book rows that all checkers FAIL.
