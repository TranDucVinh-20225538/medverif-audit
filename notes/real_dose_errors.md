## Caveats

- Automatic flags compare the answer's first dose with the doses of ONE label sentence; answers that are right for another population/indication in the excerpt are `match_other_context` and excluded; per-day vs per-dose (`time_ambiguous`) and quantity kinds absent from the target (`unverifiable`) are excluded.
- Closed-book answers are checked against the label excerpt; a closed-book 'error' may be a legitimate dose from another label/regimen (human column decides).
- Checker verdicts use each checker's default operating point (the paper's: MiniCheck prob>0.5, AlignScore >0.5, judges' parsed verdicts).
