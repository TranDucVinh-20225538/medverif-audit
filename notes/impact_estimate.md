Generated by `scripts/journal2/impact_estimate.py` from the real-dose outputs (`results/journal2/real_dose/`); JSON: `results/journal2/impact/impact.json`. **Error labels are automatic (rule-based, `rd_extract.py`) and pending human verification in `qc/real_dose_errors_check.csv` (0 rows labelled so far).**

Denominator: 1451 answers that state a dose (5 no-dose answers excluded; 197 drugs, 364 questions, 2 generators x grounded/closed-book). Checker = PASS at the paper's operating point (as `rd_report.py`). Slips = erroneous answers the checker PASSes, per 1000 dose answers scored by that checker. 95% CIs: drug-clustered bootstrap (10000 resamples).

## Paper-ready sentences (automatic labels; update after human verification)

- Of every 1000 dose answers produced by gpt-4o-mini and gemma-3-27b, 96 (95% CI 77-116) contained an automatically flagged dose error: 180 (145-216) when answering closed-book versus 14 (5-25) when grounded in the FDA label excerpt.
- At the paper's operating points, the 7 fully scored checkers let 6.2-13.1 of these erroneous answers per 1000 through, 4.8-10.3 of them unit-type errors (wrong unit or dosing basis): unit-type errors are 34% of flagged errors, but each checker passed 15-32% of them versus 1-6% of value errors.
- 14 of the 47 unit-type flags are suspected label-parsing artifacts (e.g. `mg/m 2` from a lost superscript); excluding them, unit-type errors fall to 22.7 per 1000 answers and total slips to 3.4-8.3 per 1000, so the unit-type figures (especially grounded) are upper bounds until `qc/real_dose_errors_check.csv` is adjudicated.

## Caveats

- Automatic labels compare the answer's first dose with one label sentence; `match_other_context`, `time_ambiguous` and `unverifiable` answers count as non-errors in the main denominator (error rate is a lower bound for those, an upper bound for artifact-prone unit flags). Closed-book 'errors' may be legitimate doses from another label/regimen.
- Missing judges are skipped and picked up automatically on rerun (MedScore RD scores: HPC job 62101). Judges with incomplete coverage (RAGAS Llama-3.3-70B: see coverage column and missing-by-cell line) use their scored rows only; the post-stratified overall reweights generator x mode cells to full size.
- Per-1000 figures are specific to this question set (FDA-label [DOSE] cloze questions over 197 drugs), not a general clinical error rate.
